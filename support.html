Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open PoolPal and go to the Pro / subscription screen, then tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID / Google account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PoolPal → Cancel. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → PoolPal → Cancel. Your Pro access remains until the end of the current billing period.

Where is my data stored?

All of your pool and test data is stored only on your device. Deleting the App deletes this data. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I export my history?

Use the Export / CSV option in the App to save or share your test history.
