Privacy Policy — PoolPal

Effective date: October 16, 2026

PoolPal ("the App") is a pool water-care log and chemical dosing calculator. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Data stored on your device

Your pool profiles, water test readings, dosing history, and reminder settings are stored locally on your device in the App's private database. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Purchases

PoolPal offers an optional Pro subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and managed through RevenueCat. We do not see or store your payment details. RevenueCat processes purchase information (such as product identifiers and transaction status) as needed to verify your subscription; see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Apple's and Google's handling of purchase data is governed by their own privacy policies.

What we do not collect

  • No user accounts — the App does not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • No advertising — the App contains no ads and no advertising trackers.
  • No analytics SDKs.
  • No location data.

Notifications

The App can schedule local reminders on your device (e.g., a daily water-test reminder). These are generated entirely on-device.

Children

PoolPal is intended for adults (pool owners/operators) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the App. If you subscribed to Pro, manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store / Google Play account settings; cancellation does not delete on-device data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted at this URL with the effective date above.
